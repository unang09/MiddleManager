## Page body

### MiddleManager 🖕

Every day, millions of people endure frozen screens, surprise updates, and
soul-crushing load times — and are expected to just click **Shut Down** like a
civilised person.

Nobody feels good about that. Nobody.

MiddleManager fixes this.

It sits silently in your system tray, watches your webcam on demand, and shuts
your computer down when you flip it off. No buttons. No menus. Just you, your
finger, and zero regrets.

---

### How it works

1. Run MiddleManager — it hides in your system tray
2. Press `Ctrl+Alt+M` to wake the camera for 10 seconds
3. Flip off your webcam
4. Watch the 5-second countdown
5. Cancel if you've had a change of heart *(you won't)*
6. Your laptop shuts down

The camera only turns on when you press the hotkey. It is not watching you the
rest of the time. It has better things to do, and so do you.

---

### Features

- **Lives in the tray** — no window, no clutter, no evidence
- **On-demand camera** — activates on the hotkey, not 24/7
- **Global hotkey** — `Ctrl+Alt+M` works from anywhere, whatever app you're in
- **Two modes** — Manual (anytime) or Schedule (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm only)
- **Start with Windows** — so it's always ready when you are
- **5-second countdown** with a cancel button, for the weak of heart
- **Test mode** — `--test` runs the whole thing and reports the gesture instead
  of shutting down, for when you want to be sure before you're sure

---

### Two modes

**Manual** — the hotkey works anytime, any day. Maximum chaos.

**Schedule** — the hotkey only works Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Outside those
hours, pressing `Ctrl+Alt+M` does nothing. For when you want MiddleManager to
understand work-life balance better than your employer does.

---

### About that Windows warning

Windows will tell you this app is unrecognised and ask if you're sure.

It's right to be suspicious and wrong about the conclusion. The app is unsigned
because a code-signing certificate costs a few hundred dollars a year and this
is a free joke about flipping off a laptop. The maths did not work out.

Click **More info → Run anyway**, or don't — the source is on GitHub and you're
welcome to read every line and build it yourself. That's genuinely the better
option if you're unsure. It is, after all, an app whose entire purpose is
shutting your computer down. A little paranoia is healthy.
